# 🏠   Real Estate — 20 AI Agents

Real Estate is a strong domain for students because it combines **property data, listings, customer 360, market analytics, valuation, leases, documents, maintenance, tenant operations, investment analytics, geospatial data, and RAG**.

|  # | AI Agent                                       | What the Agent Does                                                                                    | Key Databricks Capability |
| -: | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
|  1 | 🏠 **Property Search Agent**                   | Finds properties based on location, budget, property type, amenities and customer preferences          | RAG + AI Search           |
|  2 | 🔎 **Property Intelligence Agent**             | Creates a 360° view of a property using listings, transactions, history and market data                | SQL + RAG                 |
|  3 | 💰 **Property Valuation Agent**                | Analyzes comparable properties, market trends and property characteristics to support valuation        | ML + Analytics            |
|  4 | 📈 **Real Estate Market Intelligence Agent**   | Analyzes property prices, demand, supply and market trends                                             | SQL + Time Series         |
|  5 | 👤 **Buyer 360 Agent**                         | Unifies buyer searches, preferences, interactions, finances and property interests                     | Customer 360              |
|  6 | 🎯 **Property Recommendation Agent**           | Recommends properties based on buyer preferences and behavior                                          | Recommendation + AI       |
|  7 | 📜 **Lease Intelligence Agent**                | Extracts rent, renewal dates, obligations, clauses and penalties from lease agreements                 | Document AI + RAG         |
|  8 | 🧾 **Contract Review Agent**                   | Reviews purchase, lease and property-management contracts                                              | Document AI + RAG         |
|  9 | 🏢 **Tenant Support Agent**                    | Handles tenant questions about leases, payments, maintenance and property services                     | RAG + Tool Calling        |
| 10 | 🔧 **Property Maintenance Agent**              | Manages maintenance requests and prioritizes repairs based on urgency and asset history                | Agent + Workflow          |
| 11 | 🏗️ **Construction Progress Agent**            | Monitors construction milestones, schedules, costs and project progress                                | Streaming + Analytics     |
| 12 | 💵 **Rental Pricing Agent**                    | Analyzes market demand, location, property features and historical rents to support pricing            | ML + Optimization         |
| 13 | 🏦 **Mortgage Intelligence Agent**             | Analyzes mortgage applications, property information and financial documents for decision support      | Document AI + RAG         |
| 14 | 📊 **Real Estate Investment Agent**            | Evaluates properties using rental income, expenses, valuation and investment metrics                   | SQL + Financial Analytics |
| 15 | 🌍 **Location Intelligence Agent**             | Analyzes geography, nearby amenities, transportation, demographics and market conditions               | Geospatial + Analytics    |
| 16 | 🏢 **Property Portfolio Agent**                | Monitors a portfolio of properties, occupancy, rent, expenses and returns                              | SQL + Customer 360        |
| 17 | 📉 **Tenant Churn Agent**                      | Identifies tenants at risk of leaving and analyzes potential retention drivers                         | ML + Customer 360         |
| 18 | ⚠️ **Property Risk Agent**                     | Identifies property risks using location, historical incidents, maintenance and market information     | ML + Geospatial           |
| 19 | 📢 **Real Estate Marketing Agent**             | Creates property insights and analyzes campaign, lead and listing performance                          | RAG + Analytics           |
| 20 | 🤖 **Real Estate Operations Supervisor Agent** | Coordinates property search, valuation, leasing, tenants, maintenance, investment and portfolio agents | Multi-Agent + Supervisor  |
