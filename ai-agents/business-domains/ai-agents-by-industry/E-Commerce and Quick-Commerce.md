## 2. Key Business Use Cases

### 2.1 Intelligent Search and Personalized Shopping

Help customers discover relevant products based on their interests, shopping behavior, and immediate needs.

- Analyze real-time clicks, searches, views, and cart events.
- Generate personalized product recommendations.
- Improve search relevance using customer intent and product attributes.
- Recommend products based on local availability.

**Key KPIs:** Search conversion rate, zero-result rate, recommendation conversion rate, average order value, and revenue per session.

### 2.2 Demand Forecasting and Planning

Forecast demand by product, location, and time period to improve product availability.

- Account for local festivals, regional holidays, and long weekends.
- Analyze seasonality, promotions, and historical sales.
- Plan inventory for expected demand spikes.
- Forecast demand at warehouse, dark-store, and delivery-zone levels.

**Key KPIs:** Forecast accuracy, forecast bias, stockout rate, and fill rate.

### 2.3 Inventory and Stock Optimization

Maintain sufficient stock while reducing excess inventory, expiry losses, and working capital requirements.

- Prevent stockouts and overstocks.
- Track inventory across warehouses, dark stores, and small hubs.
- Plan replenishment using demand, supplier lead times, and shelf life.
- Prioritize **First Expiry, First Out (FEFO)** for perishable products.
- Identify slow-moving and near-expiry stock for transfers or markdowns.

**Key KPIs:** Inventory turnover, days of inventory, stockout rate, expiry waste, and inventory accuracy.

### 2.4 Customer 360 and Segmentation

Build a unified customer view to understand purchasing behavior and engagement.

- Identify high-value customers and new customers.
- Detect customers who have become inactive over defined periods.
- Segment customers by purchase frequency, spending, preferences, and location.
- Analyze customer journeys across browsing, purchasing, delivery, and support.

**Key KPIs:** Repeat purchase rate, purchase frequency, customer lifetime value, churn rate, and reactivation rate.

### 2.5 Returns, Refunds, and Reverse Logistics

Manage post-purchase issues and identify their root causes.

**Common issues:**

- Shipping delays or failed deliveries.
- Incorrect or damaged products.
- Missing items or quantities.
- Partial deliveries across multiple shipments.

**Return classifications:**

- Product quality issues.
- Supplier or seller issues.
- Warehouse picking and packing errors.
- Delivery-related issues.
- Customer-initiated reasons.

**Key KPIs:** Return rate, refund cycle time, reverse-logistics cost, and repeat complaint rate.

### 2.6 Fraud Detection and Risk Management

Identify suspicious activity across the commerce ecosystem.

- Customer payment, account, refund, and promotion abuse.
- Delivery-related discrepancies and false delivery confirmations.
- Shipment diversion or manipulation.
- Dark-store inventory theft and stock adjustments.
- Supplier billing, quantity, and quality discrepancies.

**Key KPIs:** Fraud loss rate, chargeback rate, false-positive rate, and investigation turnaround time.

### 2.7 Last-Mile Delivery Optimization

Improve delivery speed, reliability, and cost.

- Optimize routes using distance, traffic, and delivery commitments.
- Allocate riders based on availability, capacity, location, and vehicle suitability.
- Batch compatible orders.
- Predict delays and update estimated delivery times.

**Key KPIs:** On-time delivery rate, cost per delivery, rider utilization, deliveries per hour, and failed delivery rate.

### 2.8 Dark-Store and Small-Hub Optimization

Monitor fulfillment operations in real time.

- Track stock availability and incoming orders.
- Measure order confirmation, picking, packing, and rider handover times.
- Identify bottlenecks and staffing requirements.
- Improve product placement for faster picking.

**Key KPIs:** Order processing time, picking accuracy, packing time, orders per labor hour, and order cancellation rate.

### 2.9 Customer Loyalty and Retention

Encourage repeat purchases through relevant offers and reliable service.

- Provide personalized discounts and loyalty rewards.
- Send birthday and anniversary offers where customers have opted in.
- Run reactivation campaigns for inactive customers.
- Send relevant replenishment and back-in-stock notifications.
- Measure whether offers generate additional purchases.

**Key KPIs:** Retention rate, repeat purchase rate, loyalty participation, offer redemption, and incremental campaign revenue.

### 2.10 Pricing, Cost Optimization, and Profitability

Balance customer demand, product availability, and business economics.

- Adjust pricing and promotions based on demand, competition, and inventory.
- Reduce unnecessary discounts on products with strong demand.
- Use targeted markdowns for slow-moving and near-expiry products.
- Analyze product, order, store, and delivery-zone profitability.
- Include procurement, fulfillment, delivery, payment, return, and marketing costs.

**Key KPIs:** Gross margin, contribution margin per order, fulfillment cost, discount rate, and promotion ROI.

> **Illustrative assumption:** A business might model gross margins of 40% for one product and 10% for another. These are scenario inputs, not standard industry margins. Gross margin does not equal net profit.

## 3. Master Data and Inventory Management

Maintain consistent master data for:

- Products, SKUs, categories, and brands.
- Suppliers and sellers.
- Customers and delivery addresses.
- Warehouses, dark stores, and delivery zones.
- Prices, promotions, and product shelf-life rules.

Use operational inventory data to track:

- Batch numbers and expiry dates.
- Stock quantities and inventory aging.
- Slow-moving and near-expiry products.
- Festival-sale inventory and markdown eligibility.

**Key distinction:** Product definitions belong in master data; stock movements, batch balances, and expiry monitoring also require operational inventory records.

## 4. Funding Models and Business Priorities

| Funding Model | Potential Priorities | Measures to Monitor |
|---|---|---|
| Parent-company funding | Expansion, customer acquisition, shared infrastructure, and cross-business benefits | Growth, contribution margin, cash consumption, and strategic value |
| Investor funding | Customer acquisition, market expansion, repeat usage, and a path to sustainable economics | CAC, retention, LTV, burn rate, runway, and contribution margin |
| Founder or self-funding | Cash discipline, targeted marketing, controlled discounts, and sustainable growth | Cash flow, payback period, operating costs, and profitability |

**Planning considerations:**

- Some businesses accept early losses while building their customer base.
- The investment horizon depends on funding agreements and business performance.
- Customer acquisition does not automatically lead to profitability.
- Margin assumptions such as **below 10%, 10%, or 15–20%** must specify whether they refer to gross, contribution, or net margin.

## 5. Platform Engagement and Revenue: Hypotheses to Validate

Changes in platform usage can influence advertising opportunities, customer acquisition, and revenue. The following are research questions rather than established conclusions.

| Hypothesis | Evidence Required |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn engagement may be declining among a particular audience | Active users, posting frequency, engagement, audience segment, geography, and time period |
| X may serve as an alternative for some professional discussions | Professional audience overlap, content engagement, lead quality, and conversion |
| Facebook and Instagram may attract different age groups | Age-segmented usage, geography, engagement, and advertising performance |
| Lower engagement may affect platform revenue | Advertising impressions, pricing, subscriptions, conversion, and revenue mix |

**Terminology:** Meta is the company associated with Facebook and Instagram; “metadata” means data describing other data.

Avoid assuming that one generation uses only one platform or that declining engagement automatically produces an equivalent decline in revenue.
