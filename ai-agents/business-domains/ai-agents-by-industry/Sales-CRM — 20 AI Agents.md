# 💼   Sales / CRM — 20 AI Agents

Sales / CRM is an excellent **Agentic AI + Databricks** domain because it combines **customer 360, leads, opportunities, sales pipelines, account intelligence, forecasting, customer interactions, recommendations, pricing, contracts, and revenue analytics**.

|  # | AI Agent                                 | What the Agent Does                                                                             | Key Databricks Capability     |
| -: | ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
|  1 | 🎯 **Lead Qualification Agent**          | Scores and prioritizes leads using profile, behavior, engagement and historical conversion data | ML + Customer 360             |
|  2 | 🔎 **Sales Research Agent**              | Researches prospects, companies, industries, products and business signals                      | RAG + AI Search               |
|  3 | 👤 **Account Intelligence Agent**        | Creates a 360° view of accounts, contacts, opportunities, interactions and business activity    | Customer 360 + SQL            |
|  4 | 📈 **Sales Pipeline Agent**              | Monitors pipeline stages, deal movement, aging and conversion rates                             | SQL + Analytics               |
|  5 | 🔮 **Sales Forecasting Agent**           | Forecasts bookings and revenue based on pipeline, historical performance and deal signals       | ML + Time Series              |
|  6 | 🤝 **Opportunity Intelligence Agent**    | Analyzes opportunities and identifies deal risks, gaps and next actions                         | RAG + SQL                     |
|  7 | 🚨 **Deal Risk Agent**                   | Detects stalled deals, missing stakeholders, pricing concerns and competitive risks             | ML + Analytics                |
|  8 | 📝 **Meeting Intelligence Agent**        | Summarizes sales meetings and extracts action items, requirements and commitments               | NLP + RAG                     |
|  9 | 📧 **Sales Engagement Agent**            | Recommends personalized follow-ups and engagement actions based on account context              | RAG + Customer 360            |
| 10 | 🎯 **Next-Best-Action Agent**            | Recommends the next action a salesperson should take for an account or opportunity              | ML + Agent                    |
| 11 | 💰 **Pricing & Discount Agent**          | Analyzes pricing, discounts, deal size and historical outcomes to support pricing decisions     | Analytics + Optimization      |
| 12 | 🏆 **Cross-Sell & Upsell Agent**         | Identifies existing customers likely to purchase additional products or services                | Recommendation + Customer 360 |
| 13 | 📉 **Customer Churn Agent**              | Predicts customer churn and identifies potential retention actions                              | ML + Customer 360             |
| 14 | 📊 **Sales Performance Agent**           | Analyzes sales-rep, territory, product and regional performance                                 | SQL + BI                      |
| 15 | 🌎 **Territory Intelligence Agent**      | Identifies market opportunities, territory gaps and account potential                           | Geospatial + Analytics        |
| 16 | 🏢 **Competitive Intelligence Agent**    | Tracks competitors, products, pricing, market activity and competitive threats                  | RAG + AI Search               |
| 17 | 📑 **Sales Contract Agent**              | Reviews contracts, pricing clauses, SLAs, renewal dates and obligations                         | Document AI + RAG             |
| 18 | 🔄 **Renewal Intelligence Agent**        | Identifies upcoming renewals and predicts renewal probability                                   | ML + Customer 360             |
| 19 | 💵 **Revenue Intelligence Agent**        | Analyzes bookings, ARR/MRR, pipeline, conversion and revenue performance                        | SQL + Financial Analytics     |
| 20 | 🤖 **Sales Operations Supervisor Agent** | Coordinates lead, account, opportunity, forecasting, pricing, competitive and renewal agents    | Multi-Agent + Supervisor      |

# 🎓 Student Progression

| Level               | Projects                                                                              | Skills                          |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| 🟢 **Beginner**     | Sales Analytics, Sales Research, Account Intelligence, Contract, Meeting Intelligence | RAG + SQL                       |
| 🟡 **Intermediate** | Lead Qualification, Pipeline, Opportunity, Engagement, Renewal                        | Customer 360 + Tool Calling     |
| 🟠 **Advanced**     | Churn, Forecasting, Cross-Sell, Pricing, Deal Risk                                    | ML + Analytics                  |
| 🔴 **Expert**       | Territory Intelligence, Competitive Intelligence, Next-Best-Action                    | Recommendation + Optimization   |
| 🏆 **Capstone**     | **Sales Operations Supervisor**                                                       | Multi-Agent + Human-in-the-Loop |

### Ideal learning sequence

**Sales Research → RAG Agent → SQL Sales Analytics → Account 360 → Lead Qualification → Pipeline Intelligence → Opportunity Risk → Forecasting → Next-Best-Action → Multi-Agent Sales Supervisor**
